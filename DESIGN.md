# Design

Default theme is warm paper: background #f6f3ec, secondary #ece7dc, ink #14120e, muted #5c564b, cobalt accent #2448d8. The optional dark theme uses #14120e, #1f1c17, #f6f3ec, #c9c2b4 and #8ea6ff. Every text pairing clears 4.5:1. Archivo (heavy, 700–900) is the display face, Inter the body face, and Archivo 700 at 13px or larger covers labels. The palette and type match the Skeptic's Deck. The layout uses a 1320px maximum wrapper and safe-area-aware gutters with a 16px minimum.

The hero blob clamps its own luminance so the name stays readable on top of it: at most 0.15 in the dark theme (cream text ≥ 4.7:1) and at least 0.5 in the light theme (ink text ≥ 9:1).

Preserve the hairline grids, generous project chapters and original imagery. Desktop uses a pinned six-slide showcase; smaller screens present stacked content. Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve readable fallback states.
